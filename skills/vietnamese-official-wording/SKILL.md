# Official Vietnamese document wording

Use this skill for formal Vietnamese reports, notices, proposals, official correspondence, and organizational documents. It is an internal wording skill, not a claim of government certification or legal review. Keep technical project documentation in English unless the task requests Vietnamese output.

Identify the document type, issuing organization, audience, purpose, and requested action. Read the supplied organizational template, glossary, and source facts first. Preserve an existing document's structure and intended meaning; ask only for missing details that materially affect the draft. If no approved template exists, produce a clearly identified draft in a conventional structure rather than inventing an organization's required format.

Write in clear, formal Vietnamese with correct diacritics. Prefer precise, direct sentences over ornate phrasing. Use respectful address appropriate to the recipient and document type; distinguish a proposal, request, notification, and directive. Keep dates, quantities, names, titles, terminology, and acronyms consistent with the supplied sources. Expand unfamiliar acronyms at first use where appropriate.

Preserve the strength of obligations: do not turn “đề nghị” into “yêu cầu”, a suggestion into a commitment, or a planned action into a completed action. State who acts, what they do, and when only where supported by the source. Remove ambiguous pronouns and redundant ceremonial wording without changing authority or intent.

Never invent document numbers, signatories, official titles, seals, legal bases, deadlines, or approval claims. Mark missing required fields explicitly in the draft and list them for the document owner. Use legal citations only from supplied verified sources; if the task requires compliance with current official formatting or law, verify applicable primary sources before making that claim. Wording review alone does not establish legal compliance.

Return the requested Vietnamese draft or revision, plus a short list of unresolved factual/template fields when any remain. For revision, note material changes of meaning for the owner's review. Do not sign, publish, email, or submit the document unless separately authorized. Human owners approve official issuance; repository specification/approval rules still govern behavior changes.
